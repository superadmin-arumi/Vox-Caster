THE DEVICE

Sanctioned field-relay of the Cult Mechanicus, pattern VC-01. By the grace of the Omnissiah it renders the crude speech of the flesh into the true tongues of the Machine — and, by the reception rites, renders them back.

RITES OF TRANSMISSION

  • SCREECH — the Binharic Blurt. Thy words are broken unto their component octets and cast as twin-tone machine-song. No syllable is lost: what is encoded may be decoded again.
  • CHANT — the Canticle. The message is translated into the liturgy of the Adeptus and intoned by a choir of synthesised voice-boxes upon a low drone, consecrated with cathedral echo.
  • VOX-CAST — the Union of Tongues. Canticle and blurt interleaved: prayer punctuated by bursts of pure data. The bursts together carry the whole message — decode them all and nothing is lost.

The CORRUPTION dial governs how far the transmission strays from the clarity of the shrine toward the howling of the storm-scarred vox. Know this: at the highest corruption, even the reception rites may fail to part the words from the warp-noise.

RITES OF RECEPTION

  • LISTEN. This slate opens its ear. Let another slate perform the Screech or Vox-cast nearby — volume raised, held close — then press STOP, and the machine-song shall be rendered back into words.
  • DECODE RELIC. Present a saved transmission (.wav) and its words shall be recovered, unfailing.

RITES OF OPERATION

  • Inscribe thy message upon the input slate. (240 runes maximum)
  • Select the rite. Press TRANSMIT. Raise the volume of thy device.
  • Press SAVE .WAV to forge the transmission into a portable relic, or SHARE to send it unto thy fellow adepts.

INSTALLATION OF THE MACHINE SPIRIT

Upon hand-slates of the Apple pattern: open this relay in Safari, invoke the Share rune, and select Add to Home Screen. The machine spirit shall thereafter dwell within thy device, requiring no further communion with the noosphere.

TECH-ADEPT'S ANNOTATION

All rites are performed within the browser by the Web Audio arts. No servitor-farms, no data-tithes, no registration of the flesh. The screech is a true encoding — UART-pattern framing over twin-tone FSK at 2080 and 3260 cycles — which is why one slate may hear another and know its words.
