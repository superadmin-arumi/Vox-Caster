# ++ VOX-CASTER ++

*Sanctioned field-relay of the Cult Mechanicus, pattern VC-01. By the grace of the Omnissiah it renders the crude speech of the flesh into the true tongues of the Machine.*

**⛭ INVOKE THE RELAY:** https://superadmin-arumi.github.io/Vox-Caster/

---

## ++ RITES OF TRANSMISSION ++

The device performs three sanctioned rites:

- **SCREECH — the Binharic Blurt.** The supplicant's words are broken unto their component octets and cast as twin-tone machine-song, wreathed in blessed static and the shrieking of data. No syllable is lost: what is encoded may, by the proper rites, be decoded again.
- **CHANT — the Canticle.** The message is first translated into the liturgy of the Adeptus, then intoned by a choir of synthesised voice-boxes, sustained upon a low drone and consecrated with cathedral echo.
- **VOX-CAST — the Union of Tongues.** Canticle and blurt interleaved, as the tech-priests converse among themselves: prayer punctuated by bursts of pure data.

The **CORRUPTION** dial governs how far the transmission strays from the clarity of the shrine toward the howling of the storm-scarred vox.

## ++ RITES OF OPERATION ++

1. Inscribe thy message upon the input slate. *(240 runes maximum)*
2. Select the rite of transmission.
3. Press **TRANSMIT**. Raise the volume of thy device, that the Omnissiah may be heard.
4. Press **SAVE AUDIO** to forge the transmission into a portable relic (.wav).

## ++ INSTALLATION OF THE MACHINE SPIRIT ++

Upon hand-slates of the Apple pattern: open the relay in Safari, invoke the **Share** rune, and select **Add to Home Screen**. The machine spirit shall thereafter dwell within thy device, requiring no further communion with the noosphere.

## ++ TECH-ADEPT'S ANNOTATION ++

All rites are performed within the browser by the Web Audio arts. No servitor-farms, no data-tithes, no registration of the flesh. The screech is a true encoding — UART-pattern framing over twin-tone FSK — and awaits the decoder-rite of a future consecration, whereby one hand-slate may hear another and render the machine-song back into words.
